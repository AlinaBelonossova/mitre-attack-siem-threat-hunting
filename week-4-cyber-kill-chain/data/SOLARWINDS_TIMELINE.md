# SolarWinds Compromise — Attack Timeline

## 2019

MITRE ATT&CK records the SolarWinds Compromise campaign as first seen in August 2019.

## February 2020

Microsoft reporting indicates that the malicious Solorigate/SUNBURST component was fully functional by late February 2020.

## March 2020

The compromised component was incorporated into SolarWinds Orion software and began being distributed through legitimate software updates.

## June 2020

Microsoft reported that the backdoor code was removed from the build environment.

## 2020

The campaign continued with selective victim targeting and post-compromise activity. SUNBURST contacted attacker-controlled infrastructure and selected some victims for additional activity.

## December 2020

The campaign became publicly known after discovery of the SolarWinds-related compromise.

## January 2021

Microsoft published a detailed analysis of the second-stage activation process, including SUNBURST, TEARDROP/Raindrop, and follow-on activity.

## April 2021

The United States and United Kingdom publicly attributed the SolarWinds campaign to Russia's SVR, as recorded by MITRE ATT&CK.
